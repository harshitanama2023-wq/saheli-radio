Ek radio, sab saheliyon ke liye

Saheli Radio is a free music corner for working women and girls — for the ten-minute dance break between meetings, the hostel room party, the sangeet rehearsal, and the late-night deadline that needs lofi. Pick a mood, press play, and it keeps going on phone or laptop. No signup, no app.

Songs play through YouTube’s official embedded player, so every play counts for the artists and labels who made them.

Sawaal-jawaab

Do I need an account?

No. Open the page and press play.

Where does the music come from?

From public YouTube playlists, played in YouTube’s own player. You can open any mood on YouTube with the arrow button.

What is the dance break timer?

Pick 5, 10 or 15 minutes. When time is up the music pauses so you remember to get back to work (or not — we won’t tell).

Can I play my own songs?

Yes. Paste any public YouTube playlist link into “Play your own playlist”.

A song got skipped — why?

Some videos don’t allow playing on other websites. Saheli Radio skips those and plays the next one.
